Blueprint Resident Instructional Education Framework

Every good session starts with a B.R.I.E.F. It walks you through your teaching plan step by step: objectives, scenario, delivery framework, take-home messages. You bring the clinical knowledge. We give you the structure to plan it and the prompts to execute it.

You stay in controlYou decide what to teach. The tool never generates clinical content on your behalf.
7 steps, under 10 minutesWalk through audience, topic, objectives, key points, scenario, understanding check, and take-home messages. Choose your teaching approach directly on the plan.
A plan you can print and useThe output gives you a complete, structured session plan ready to take into the clinical environment.

Set the scene

A realistic ward scenario to anchor the session. Two or three sentences. Start with the patient and the problem, not the diagnosis.

Keep it anonymous: Use fictional ages, initials, or composite details. Never include anything that could identify a real patient.
